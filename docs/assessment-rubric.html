Questions for a short discussion

In your cleaning task, which records were excluded and why did you keep the missing-campus order?

C003 has unparseable quantity text, C005 has no unit price, and C006 has zero quantity under the stated positive-quantity rule. C007 can still contribute known revenue; its campus is marked Unknown and flagged as originally missing. Different analysis questions may require different completeness rules.

Your outer merge has nine rows, although there were only eight orders. Where did the extra row come from?

Juice occurs only in the menu. An outer merge preserves it as a right_only row, which is not an order. Sandwich creates two left_only order rows because it is missing from the menu. The merge did not invent a ninth transaction.

Why did you use pivot_table instead of pivot for the revenue matrix?

Some Campus-Item combinations have multiple orders. pivot requires unique row-column combinations, while pivot_table can aggregate them using sum. The chosen aggregation answers total-revenue questions.

RAK has higher revenue in these records. Does that prove its café is more profitable or that the difference will continue?

No. Revenue is not profit; costs are absent. These are only eight fictional orders over four days, so they do not support a real business conclusion or forecast.
