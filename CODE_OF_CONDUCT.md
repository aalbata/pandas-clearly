# Learning and working together

Pandas, Clearly welcomes learners and contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Ask questions, share corrections, and give others room to learn.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss code, evidence, and explanations respectfully.
- Assume that a question may come from someone encountering the idea for the first time.
- Give feedback that identifies the problem and helps someone make progress.
- Acknowledge others' contributions and preserve source attribution.
- Respect people's privacy and avoid posting personal information in examples or issues.

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, personal attacks, threats, and repeated disruption are not acceptable. Criticism of an idea is welcome; attacks on a person are not.

## Raising a concern

For a content or collaboration problem that can be discussed publicly, open an issue with a factual description and relevant links. If a concern involves sensitive personal information, use an established private contact route with the maintainer rather than posting that information publicly.

The maintainer may request changes, moderate a discussion, close an issue, or restrict participation when necessary to keep the project useful and respectful. Decisions should be proportionate to the conduct and the circumstances.
